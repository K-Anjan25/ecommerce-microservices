# Cartly new visual direction

Selected by the owner on 2026-08-23: **A — Editorial Warmth**.

The store is now positioned as a **premium curated lifestyle marketplace**, not a dense general marketplace. Direction:

- warm cream, espresso and rust;
- typographic wordmark rather than an app-icon logo;
- Instrument Serif for editorial display and Inter for commerce UI;
- asymmetrical image-led hero;
- unboxed product cards and circular category photography;
- search remains functional but visually secondary to collection storytelling.

`concept-a-editorial.jpg` is the selected mood concept. Concepts B and C remain decision history, not implementation specifications. Generated concept imagery is exploratory and must not be shipped as product photography.
