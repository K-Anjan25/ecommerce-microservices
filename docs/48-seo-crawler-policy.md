# SEO crawler policy

`frontend/public/robots.txt` now allows public catalog/storefront discovery and disallows private, transactional, payment, account, guest-capability, and admin routes.

The policy intentionally disallows `/guest-order` and `/order-confirmation` because those routes can carry order-specific information. Gift-card wallet, checkout, account and operational screens are also private experiences.

This improves crawler hygiene but does not replace SSR or pre-rendering. Product and storefront metadata is still generated by the existing client-side metadata hook; a production deployment still needs a server-rendered or pre-rendered public catalog for dependable crawler indexing.
